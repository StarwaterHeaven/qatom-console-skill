# Changelog

## 0.2 — 10 October 2026

Rewritten from a guided walk-through of the TODAQ Console on a live customer twin group.
- How a sale moves: provider twin paywall → Qatom fulfilment → standing distribution → primary → payouts / cash-out.
- TODA file types (DQ notes, SSR binder, SDA identity root); notes split and give change like cash.
- Sign-in, roles (Admin, Transfer, Read-only, group Manager) and the re-sign-in rule.
- Every Wallet and Configuration page: Inventory, Transactions, Transfer Activity, Send, Distributions (≤ 200 payees, email payees, CSV), Paywall and Directory, Payment Multiplex (payers, daily top-ups from the master), Buy TDN (Treasury requests), Banking Profile and Bank Transfer (≥ 10 TDN, flat 1.00 fee, submits on Continue), Twin Settings, Usage.
- Recipes for the common owner questions.
